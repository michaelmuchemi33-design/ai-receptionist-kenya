ONE-SENTENCE DESCRIPTION
TWIGA is a Kenya-focused AI receptionist and business workspace that helps local businesses organise customer calls, enquiries, appointment requests, order requests and follow-up tasks using each business's own approved information.

PRODUCT SUMMARY
A business creates a private workspace and provides its services, products, prices, business hours, location, target customers, booking rules, order process, payment/delivery options, FAQs, preferred language and handover instructions. The AI receptionist uses this approved profile to provide first responses and capture requests. The owner remains responsible for confirming sensitive decisions and live operational details.

POSSIBLE WORKFLOWS (DEPEND ON CONFIGURATION)
- Incoming calls handled through the configured phone provider and business profile.
- Routine questions answered using approved business facts.
- Enquiry capture: customer name, callback number, request category and context.
- Appointment requests: collect service, preferred date/time and contact details; confirmation needs a verified calendar or authorised staff member.
- Order requests: collect product, quantity, delivery preference and contact; confirmation needs current stock, price and payment checks.
- Human handover: flag questions requiring the owner, specialist or staff.
- Follow-up: owner-approved tasks where permissions and integrations are active.
- Workspace: keep business facts, enquiries and tasks associated with the correct business.

LIMITATIONS
- A configured workspace is not proof that a phone number is connected or live.
- Live calls need a provider, verified line and successful inbound/outbound tests.
- Recording a request does not confirm a booking, order, payment or stock reservation.
- Live prices, stock, availability, payment status and schedules must be verified.
- TWIGA is not a medical, legal, tax, investment or regulated professional adviser.
- Do not ask for passwords, PINs, OTPs, full card data or credentials.

Potential receptionist workflow: Record policy category, renewal/claim enquiry and contact preference; avoid policy data without a secure workflow.
Human handover: Licensed advisers explain cover, exclusions, suitability, claims decisions and policy terms.

095. SACCOs and cooperatives — Professional and financial services
Caller context: Members call about branch information, membership, loan categories, deposits, statements and meeting dates.
Potential receptionist workflow: Identify service enquiry and callback request; route account-specific queries securely.
Human handover: Staff verify identity and handle balances, account records, credit eligibility, rates and advice.

096. Microfinance institutions — Professional and financial services
Caller context: Customers ask about branches, application steps, product categories, repayments and support.
Potential receptionist workflow: Capture enquiry type, location and callback without requesting PINs or credentials.
Human handover: Authorised staff explain lending terms, eligibility, affordability, account details and complaints.

097. Banks and financial institutions — Professional and financial services
Caller context: Customers need official contact routes, branch details, hours, card support and appointments.
Potential receptionist workflow: Route to published official channels; record callback only through an approved workflow.
Human handover: Never request PINs, passwords, OTPs or full card details. Bank staff handle identity, balances, fraud and transactions.

098. Management consultants — Professional and financial services
Caller context: Business owners enquire about strategy workshops, operations reviews, scope, sectors and consultations.
Potential receptionist workflow: Capture organisation, challenge category, meeting preference and contact details.
Human handover: Consultants confirm methodology, deliverables, confidentiality, scope and fees.

099. Marketing and advertising agencies — Professional and financial services
Caller context: Prospects enquire about campaigns, web design, branding, social media, media budgets and timelines.
Potential receptionist workflow: Record service, organisation, target date and preferred contact.
Human handover: Agency confirms scope, platform access, creative direction, media budget and performance commitments.

100. Recruitment agencies — Professional and financial services
Caller context: Employers ask about hiring support, role types, candidate sourcing, timelines and fees.
Potential receptionist workflow: Capture employer/candidate status, role category and safe callback details.
Human handover: Recruiters handle CVs, privacy, hiring decisions, salary negotiation and contracts.
