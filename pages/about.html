Who we are

Technology that works alongside your team.

Running a business means balancing customers, staff and the work that keeps everything moving. Calls can arrive while your team is serving someone, handling a delivery or helping another customer.

AI Receptionist Kenya is designed to take care of the first conversation: answering common questions, sharing approved business information, collecting enquiries and helping callers reach the right next step. It is not about replacing the people who make your business special. It is about giving them more room to do their best work.

What guides the product

Useful, clear and built around real businesses.
