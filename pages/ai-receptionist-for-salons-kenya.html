AI receptionist for salons and barbers in Kenya

Give callers a helpful first response while your team concentrates on clients in the chair. TWIGA is designed around the everyday questions salons and barbers receive.

Help customers get answers while you are serving someone

When a barber is cutting hair or a stylist is working with a client, answering the phone can interrupt the appointment already in progress. The caller may only need to know your location, opening hours, service list or starting prices. An AI receptionist can be configured to answer approved routine questions and collect the details of a customer who needs a person to respond.

This is useful during busy periods, after closing time or when a small team cannot answer every call immediately. It gives the customer a clear next step without requiring staff to leave a client halfway through a service.

Common salon and barbershop enquiries

Services and prices

Share approved information about cuts, styling, braiding, colouring, treatments, grooming packages and prices or price ranges the business has confirmed.

Appointment requests

Collect the requested service, preferred day and time, customer name and contact details. A staff member or connected booking system should confirm the appointment.

Location and opening hours

Help callers find the shop, understand business hours and check whether holiday or weekend hours differ from the normal schedule.

Follow-up requests

Capture questions about availability, bridal or group bookings, products and services that need a stylist or manager to provide a specific answer.

Keep control of your booking rules

Before activation, decide which appointment requests can be recorded and which require staff approval. A regular haircut may follow a simple booking rule, while a colour treatment may need a consultation and a longer time slot. The receptionist should not promise a time that has not been checked or treat an enquiry as a confirmed booking unless the booking connection supports that action.

Set up information your team can maintain

Prepare a current service menu, prices, opening hours, location details, cancellation policy and instructions for late arrivals. Name the person who approves changes and keep a process for updating information before promotions or public holidays. Test questions using the wording your customers actually use, including the languages you expect to support, before making the service available to callers.

Support your staff, do not replace personal service

A receptionist can help with predictable first responses and enquiry capture while stylists and barbers continue to build relationships with customers. Complaints, unusual requests, sensitive matters and detailed consultations should be transferred or followed up by a person. Review call examples regularly and adjust the instructions whenever the team spots an unclear or inaccurate answer.

Frequently asked questions

Can it book appointments automatically?

It can collect appointment requests. Automatic confirmation depends on the booking system and phone configuration connected to your business, so confirm that capability before relying on it.

Can it answer questions about prices and services?

Yes, when you provide current, approved service and pricing information. Set a clear rule for packages or custom work that require a staff quotation.

Will it replace the barber or stylist?

No. It is intended to support routine first responses and enquiry capture. Your team remains responsible for the service, customer relationship and decisions that need judgement.
