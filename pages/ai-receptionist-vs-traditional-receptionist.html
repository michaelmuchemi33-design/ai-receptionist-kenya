AI Receptionist vs Traditional Receptionist

Understand where an AI receptionist can help, where a person is still essential and how the two can work together.

Routine enquiries and information

An AI receptionist can answer configured questions consistently and collect enquiry details, helping reduce interruptions for staff.

Human judgement and relationships

A person remains important for sensitive conversations, unusual requests, complaints, negotiations and decisions that require judgement or empathy.

A practical blended approach

Treat the AI as an additional first point of contact, with a clear handover route to a staff member when the conversation requires it.
