How much does an AI receptionist cost in Kenya?

Compare the published TWIGA monthly plans, understand what the subscription covers and check which telephone, call-usage or integration costs may sit outside the plan.

A practical pricing guide for Kenyan business owners · Updated 14 November 2026 · Published by TWIGA

The quick answer

TWIGA monthly subscriptions currently start at KSh 1,500 for Starter, with Professional at KSh 3,000 and Business Pro at KSh 5,000. These are subscription prices, not a promise that every phone and usage charge is included. Your actual setup may also involve telephone number rental, call charges, AI processing or optional third-party services.

What does the monthly subscription cover?

The subscription covers the selected TWIGA plan and the features described for that package. Starter focuses on essential AI receptionist support. Professional adds more tailored call flows, detailed FAQs and support for enquiry handover workflows. Business Pro is intended for more advanced call-handling configuration and business-specific workflow planning.

A package description is not the same as a guarantee that every external phone feature is available. For example, automatic appointment confirmation, call transfers, outbound calls, messaging or a connection to a booking or POS system depends on the provider, integration and configuration that have been tested for your business.

Which costs may be additional?

Your final operating cost can depend on the telephone service and integrations used with the receptionist. The following items may be charged separately, depending on your configuration and provider:

  • Telephone number rental: a fee for a number or telephone service, where applicable.
  • Incoming and outgoing calls: charges can depend on the provider, destination and call usage.
  • AI processing: voice and AI usage may carry usage-based charges.
  • Optional third-party services: external calendars, messaging, phone systems or other connected services may have their own fees.
Ask for the full picture. Before activation, ask which of these costs apply to your intended setup, how they are measured and how they will appear on your bill. TWIGA's pricing page states that applicable usage charges are explained before activation.

How to estimate the total monthly cost

Do not compare providers using the subscription figure alone. Ask for a simple estimate that separates the fixed plan from any variable usage or external service charges.

For a realistic estimate, describe the number you intend to use, whether calls are inbound or outbound, approximate call volume, the integrations you need and the actions the receptionist should complete. Ask the provider to confirm the specific setup in writing.

Questions to ask before choosing an AI receptionist

  1. Can the receptionist work with the phone number and provider I already use?
  2. Are number rental, call forwarding and incoming or outgoing call charges separate?
  3. Is AI and voice usage included, limited or billed separately?
  4. Can it transfer a caller to a staff member, and has that transfer been tested?
  5. Can it confirm bookings or only collect appointment requests?
  6. Where are call records and enquiry details stored, who can access them and how long are they retained?
  7. What happens if the phone service, internet connection or AI provider is unavailable?

Common questions about AI receptionist pricing in Kenya

Is KSh 1,500 the total monthly cost?

KSh 1,500 per month is the TWIGA Starter subscription price. Telephone number rental, incoming or outgoing call charges, AI processing and optional third-party services may add costs depending on the provider and configuration. Confirm which charges apply before activation.

Are telephone number rental and call charges included?

They are not automatically included in the monthly subscription. Number rental and call charges may be billed separately depending on your phone provider and setup. Ask for the applicable rates before activating the service.

Which TWIGA plan should a small business choose?

Starter is designed for essential first-response support. Professional adds more tailored call flows and enquiry handover support. Business Pro is for more advanced call-handling configuration and workflow planning. Compare the published features and discuss your actual call flow before selecting a plan.

Can I use my existing Kenyan phone number?

That depends on the phone provider, SIM, call-forwarding options and technical configuration. Ask for a test using the number you intend to use and confirm inbound call handling and staff handover before moving customer calls.

Does the plan automatically book appointments or take orders?

Only where the relevant booking or ordering integration is available, connected and tested. Otherwise, the receptionist should collect a request for staff to review and confirm it. Do not treat an enquiry as a confirmed booking or order.
