AI receptionist for Kenyan businesses

AI receptionist for restaurants and cafés in Kenya

Help diners reach your restaurant with clear information about hours, location, menu questions and table enquiries, even when the team is busy.

Keep customer calls moving during service

Restaurants and cafés receive calls while staff are seating guests, preparing orders, serving tables and managing deliveries. A caller may be checking whether you are open, asking about a menu item or trying to arrange a table. When nobody can answer, the customer has to try again or choose another way to contact the business.

An AI receptionist can be configured to provide a first response to routine questions using information your restaurant has approved. It can also collect a request and pass it to the right team member, allowing staff to stay focused on service without losing the details of the enquiry.

Questions a restaurant receptionist can help with

Opening hours and location

Share normal hours, holiday changes, directions, parking information and the best way to reach the venue.

Menu and dietary questions

Answer only from current menu details supplied by the restaurant. Route allergy, ingredient or cross-contamination questions to staff who can verify them.

Table and group requests

Collect a preferred date, time, party size and contact number. Confirm reservations only when a connected booking process checks availability.

Takeaway and delivery enquiries

Collect the order question or request, explain approved ordering channels and direct callers to the team for details that need confirmation.

Protect accuracy for menus and reservations

Menus, prices, daily specials and availability can change. Give the receptionist a source of current information and identify what it must not guess. Ingredient and allergen questions should be handled carefully and verified by a staff member. A table request is not a confirmed reservation until the restaurant’s booking process has accepted it.

Plan a simple handover during busy periods

Agree which person checks new requests and how urgent enquiries are flagged. A useful record can include the caller’s name, phone number, party size, requested time and reason for calling. Keep only the details needed to respond, and set a realistic follow-up expectation that your staff can meet.

Start with your most frequent calls

Review a week of common questions and group them into routine information, booking requests and matters that require a person. Test the receptionist using real examples from lunch and dinner service. After launch, review handovers, update menu information and refine the instructions when the restaurant changes its hours, menu or ordering process.

Frequently asked questions

Can it confirm a table reservation?

It can collect reservation requests. Automatic confirmation requires a booking connection that can check live availability and accept the reservation; confirm this before launch.

Can it answer menu and allergen questions?

It should answer only from verified, current information. Route allergy, ingredient and cross-contamination questions to restaurant staff for confirmation.

Does it take food orders?

It can collect an order enquiry or direct callers to your approved ordering channel. Taking and confirming orders depends on the ordering or POS integration available in your setup.
