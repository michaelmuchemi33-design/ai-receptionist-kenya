One important rule: do not let automation guess.

Keep approved business information accurate, explain what the AI can and cannot do, and send uncertain, sensitive or high-impact requests to a staff member. Call transfers, bookings, messaging and other integrations depend on the provider and the configuration that has actually been tested.
