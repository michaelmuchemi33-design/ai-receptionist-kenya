AI receptionist basics

How Does an AI Receptionist Work? A Guide for Kenyan Businesses

An AI receptionist is software that can handle defined parts of a business phone conversation. It can greet callers, answer approved questions and collect enquiry details, while a person remains responsible for exceptions and important decisions.

A practical guide for Kenyan business owners · Updated 14 November 2026 · Published by TWIGA

In brief

A typical AI receptionist listens to a caller, turns speech into text, uses the business information and call instructions to prepare a response, then speaks back. Depending on the setup, it may also record an enquiry or pass the conversation to a person. The exact features depend on the phone provider, connected systems and configuration.

1. The caller reaches the configured phone service

The first step is the phone connection. A business decides which number or call flow should reach the AI receptionist. Some setups use a dedicated number, a forwarded call or a provider integration. A business should confirm how its existing number is handled before activation; an AI receptionist does not automatically take over every ordinary SIM call.

2. The receptionist identifies what the caller needs

When the caller speaks, speech recognition converts their words into information the system can process. The receptionist may identify an intent such as asking for opening hours, requesting a price, checking a service or asking for a callback. Background noise, accents, network quality and unclear speech can affect recognition, so realistic testing is important.

3. It answers from business information you approve

The business supplies a knowledge base: its name, location, hours, services, current prices, policies and common answers. Clear, current information helps the receptionist respond consistently. If a price or availability is not confirmed, the safer response is to say that a staff member needs to check rather than invent an answer.

Keep frequently changing details under review. The receptionist should know which facts it can state confidently and which requests must be escalated.

4. It collects a useful enquiry or next step

For a request that cannot be completed during the call, the system may capture the caller’s name, contact detail and reason for calling, depending on the configured workflow. Collect only details the business needs. Decide who reviews new enquiries, how quickly they should be checked and what the caller should be told about the next step.

5. A person handles exceptions and important decisions

An AI receptionist should have clear boundaries. Complaints, sensitive issues, negotiations, unusual requests and actions that need approval should go to a staff member. Call transfer, live handover, recordings and follow-up messages depend on the service provider and permissions; test each one rather than assuming it is included.

A practical example: a salon enquiry

A customer calls a salon to ask whether it offers braids and what information is needed to book. The receptionist can explain the service using the salon’s approved description, ask what appointment the customer wants and capture a request. It should not promise a slot unless it can check a current calendar or a staff member confirms the time.

What to test before using it with customers

  • Ask common questions about services, location, opening hours and prices.
  • Give it an unclear question and check whether it asks for clarification.
  • Ask for a person and verify that the agreed handover works.
  • Test a request that requires approval and confirm it does not make a promise.
  • Review how enquiries are stored, who can see them and how they are followed up.

How to prepare your business

Start with a short list of the ten questions customers ask most often. Write accurate answers, define when a human must take over and test the phone connection with your provider. You can then expand the scope after the first call flows are reliable.

For a setup checklist, read what to prepare before activation. For a comparison of staffing approaches, see AI receptionist vs traditional receptionist.

Can it answer calls on my existing Kenyan number?

That depends on the phone network, provider, device, call-forwarding options and technical setup. Ask for a demonstration using your intended number and confirm whether inbound calls, transfers and after-hours handling are supported before relying on them.

What if the AI does not know the answer?

It should say that it needs a person to confirm the information, collect the minimum details needed and follow the agreed handover process. A receptionist should never guess at prices, availability, policies or commitments.

Common questions

Does an AI receptionist replace a human receptionist?

Not necessarily. It can support routine first responses and enquiry capture, while people remain responsible for judgement, sensitive conversations and decisions that need approval.

Can an AI receptionist book appointments automatically?

Only when the setup has access to a reliable booking calendar and permission to make bookings. Otherwise, it should collect a request and let staff confirm the time.

Does the receptionist need training?

The business needs to provide and maintain accurate information, call instructions and escalation rules. Testing with real customer-style questions helps identify gaps before launch.
