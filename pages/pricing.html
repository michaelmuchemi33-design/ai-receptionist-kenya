Understand your monthly costs.

Your subscription covers your selected AI receptionist package. Telephone number rental, incoming or outgoing call charges, AI processing and optional third-party services may incur additional charges depending on your configuration and provider. Any applicable usage charges will be explained before activation so you know what to expect.

Pricing questions

Are telephone and AI usage charges included?

The monthly package covers the selected receptionist plan. Telephone number rental, call charges, AI processing and optional third-party services may be billed separately depending on your setup. We explain applicable costs before activation.

Can I change my package later?

You can discuss a package change with the team. Any change will depend on the available subscription and billing arrangements.

How do I get started?

Request a demonstration and share what your business does, the calls you receive and the kind of help you want from your receptionist.

Does choosing a plan activate the service immediately?

No. The team will confirm your requirements, provider configuration and applicable charges before activation.
