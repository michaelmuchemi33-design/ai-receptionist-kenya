Pricing questions

Are telephone and AI usage charges included?

The monthly package covers the selected receptionist plan. Telephone number rental, call charges, AI processing and optional third-party services may be billed separately depending on your setup. We explain applicable costs before activation.

Is the M-Pesa subscription payment automatic every month?

No automatic recurring debit is assumed. Each M-Pesa STK payment is a one-time payment that you approve on your phone. Your account is updated only after the payment provider verifies success.

How do I get started?

Create your account at app.twiga.co.ke. Your private business workspace is created, then the setup assistant helps configure your business. You can upload a PDF with selectable text, try the tools during the 7-day trial and choose a plan when you are ready.

When do my calls start working?

Your workspace and AI setup can be prepared during the 7-day trial. Live calls start only after a phone line is assigned or approved, the receptionist is bound to that business, call routing is verified and any required charges are approved.
