Independent Commission-Based Sales Partner

Remote · KenyaIndependent contractorCommission only

TWIGA helps businesses answer calls, capture enquiries, handle booking requests and follow up with customers using an AI receptionist.

What you'll do

  • Identify salons, clinics, restaurants, shops and other businesses that could benefit from an AI receptionist.
  • Introduce TWIGA clearly and honestly.
  • Arrange product demonstrations and follow up with interested business owners.
  • Help new customers understand onboarding and next steps.
  • Keep accurate lead notes and communicate professionally by phone and WhatsApp.

What we're looking for

  • Confident communication and relationship-building skills.
  • Self-motivation and the ability to work independently.
  • Comfort using a smartphone, WhatsApp and basic online tools.
  • Sales, field marketing or customer service experience is helpful, but not required.
  • Honesty: never promise features, prices or results that TWIGA has not confirmed.

How commission works

This is commission-only. There is no basic salary, fixed monthly pay or guaranteed income. Commission is earned only on eligible customer subscriptions successfully sold and paid for. The exact rate, qualifying rules and payment schedule will be shared in writing and agreed before you begin.

This is an independent contractor opportunity, not a salaried position.
