AI Receptionist for Hotels in Kenya: Guest Enquiries and Booking Requests

A guest may call a hotel to ask about room types, location, check-in time, facilities or availability. An AI receptionist can help answer approved general questions and capture booking enquiries, provided the hotel has a reliable process for confirming live rates and availability.

A practical guide for Kenyan business owners · Updated 14 November 2026 · Published by TWIGA

In brief

For a hotel or guest house, an AI receptionist can handle routine first responses about verified facilities, directions, check-in policies and how to request a reservation. It should only quote live rates or confirm a room when connected to an up-to-date source and authorised to complete that action.

The hotel questions callers ask first

Common enquiries include room types, check-in and check-out times, directions, parking, meals, Wi-Fi, accessibility, payment options and whether a particular date is available. A hotel can prepare short, accurate answers for questions that do not depend on live room inventory.

Separate general information from live availability

Opening hours for reception, directions and published check-in rules may be stable. Room availability, rates, promotions and booking conditions can change quickly. Keep those facts connected to the hotel's current booking system or route them to staff for confirmation. Never let the receptionist invent a rate or tell a guest that a room is reserved unless the reservation has actually been recorded.

Build a useful guest enquiry flow

  1. Ask for the intended stay dates and the type of room or request.
  2. Answer general questions using approved hotel information.
  3. Check live availability only through a verified booking integration.
  4. If no live check is available, capture a reservation enquiry for staff.
  5. Explain whether the guest has a confirmed booking or is waiting for a response.

Keep hotel information organised

  • Official property name, address and directions.
  • Room categories and accurate descriptions.
  • Check-in, check-out and reception hours.
  • Verified facility and accessibility details.
  • Cancellation, deposit and payment policies.
  • Contact routes for groups, events, complaints and special requests.

Assign an owner to update this information when seasonal offers, facilities or policies change.

Know when a human should take over

Group bookings, event arrangements, special accessibility requirements, complaints, payment disputes and unusual guest requests often need a person. Set an escalation route that reaches the right team and make sure staff know how to review enquiries that arrive outside normal hours.

Ask these questions before choosing a provider

  • Can it use the hotel's existing public number or call-forwarding setup?
  • Is there a tested connection to the property management or booking system?
  • Can it distinguish an enquiry from a confirmed reservation?
  • How are guest contact details protected and retained?
  • What happens during a network or software outage?
  • How can staff review, correct or take over a conversation?

Test with real guest scenarios

Test calls about directions, check-in time, room facilities, a date with no availability, a change request and a complaint. Verify that every answer reflects the hotel's approved information and that availability-dependent requests follow the correct process.

Measure what matters to the front desk

Review how many calls received an accurate first response, how many enquiries reached reservations staff, how often information needed correction and whether guests could reach a person. Use those findings to improve the information and handover rules.

Use automation as a front-desk support layer

The aim is to make routine information easier to access while keeping hotel staff responsible for reservations, exceptions and guest care. Start with a narrow set of verified answers and enquiry capture, then add deeper integrations only after they have been tested.

Common questions

Can an AI receptionist confirm hotel reservations?

Only when it has a tested connection to the current reservation system and permission to create bookings. Otherwise, it should record an enquiry and tell the guest that staff must confirm it.

Can it answer questions after reception closes?

That depends on the phone routing and provider setup. Confirm the after-hours call path, staff escalation and outage process before advertising 24-hour coverage.

Does it replace hotel reception staff?

It should be treated as a support layer for routine calls, not a replacement for staff who manage guest arrivals, payments, safety issues and service recovery.
