AI receptionist FAQs for Kenyan businesses

Understand what TWIGA does, who it is built for, how calls are handled and which features need a phone or software connection before they can go live.

TWIGA basics

What is TWIGA?

TWIGA is a Kenya-focused AI receptionist and business workspace. It helps a business provide a first response to customer calls using business information and call instructions approved by the owner.

What does TWIGA do?

When the phone route is configured and tested, TWIGA can greet callers, answer routine questions, capture customer enquiries, collect booking or order requests and help the team follow up. The exact actions depend on the connected phone service and enabled features.

Who is TWIGA for?

TWIGA is built for Kenyan businesses including salons, barbershops, clinics, dental practices, restaurants, cafés, hotels, guest houses, real estate agencies, retail shops, schools, professional services and field-service teams.

How does the whole process work?

The owner creates a TWIGA workspace, adds accurate business details and FAQs, sets the greeting and call rules, confirms a suitable phone connection, then tests realistic calls and handovers. Live calls begin only after the selected phone route and required features have been verified.

Calls, bookings and daily work

Can TWIGA answer calls after business hours?

It can answer after-hours calls when the phone provider and call-routing arrangement send those calls to the configured receptionist. An account by itself does not automatically redirect every business call.

Can I keep my existing Kenyan mobile or business number?

Possibly. This depends on your mobile operator, device, number type and available forwarding or provider arrangements. TWIGA does not automatically take over an ordinary SIM line just because an app is installed. Confirm the exact call path before activation.

Does TWIGA work directly with an ordinary SIM card?

Not automatically. Calls must reach a supported and configured phone service. During setup, the team should check what your current line can support and explain any provider requirements before you change your number or call routing.

Can it book confirmed appointments?

A booking is only confirmed when TWIGA has access to a current booking system and permission to reserve the slot, or when an authorised staff member confirms it. Without that connection, it captures an appointment request for the business to review.

Can it take orders and payments?

It can capture order details when that workflow is enabled. The business must still verify availability, final price, delivery details and payment status through its approved systems. Never share an M-Pesa PIN, password or one-time code with a receptionist.

Does it speak English and Kiswahili?

Language and voice support depend on the selected voice service and configuration. The business should test its preferred language, common phrases, local place names and realistic caller accents before using the receptionist with customers.

Can it transfer a call or send a request to my staff?

Human handover and notifications depend on the selected phone provider, destination numbers, permissions and integrations. Each transfer or messaging flow must be configured and tested. If a live transfer is unavailable, the agreed fallback should capture the request for staff follow-up.

Does TWIGA replace human receptionists or employees?

No. It supports routine first responses and enquiry capture so staff can focus on customers and decisions that need a person. Your team remains responsible for approvals, sensitive conversations, specialist advice and exceptions.

Does the 100-industry guide mean every feature is already active?

No. The 100 examples show where a configured receptionist may help. Phone routing, booking calendars, payments, stock, WhatsApp and other integrations are enabled only when the relevant provider and workflow are configured and tested for that business.

What happens when the AI does not know an answer?

It should not guess. The safe response is to explain that a staff member needs to confirm the detail, collect only the information needed and follow the agreed handover process.

Pricing, privacy and getting started

How much does TWIGA cost?

The website lists Starter at KSh 1,650 per month, Professional at KSh 3,000 per month and Business Pro at KSh 5,000 per month. Phone numbers, call usage, AI processing and optional third-party services may have separate charges. See the pricing page for current plan details.

How is customer information handled?

The business should collect only details needed to handle the enquiry, limit workspace access to authorised team members and follow the applicable privacy requirements. Do not ask callers for passwords, M-Pesa PINs, one-time codes or payment credentials. Read the privacy notice for website enquiry information.

Can I use this guide to build a copy of TWIGA?

The guide is written for business owners evaluating a receptionist, not as a technical build manual. It does not disclose TWIGA’s private prompts, integrations, routing configuration, source code, security design or operational playbooks. A dependable production service also requires testing, safeguards, monitoring and ongoing support.

How do I get started?

Create an account at app.twiga.co.ke, add your business information and review the available setup options. Request a demonstration to confirm your phone number, provider requirements, call flow and any additional costs before activating live calls.
