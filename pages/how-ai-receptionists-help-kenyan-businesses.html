How AI Receptionists Help Kenyan Small Businesses Answer Calls

A practical look at how an AI receptionist can support customer calls, capture enquiries and help a small team stay focused.

Start with the calls your business already receives

List the questions callers ask most often: prices, location, opening hours, availability, product details and delivery arrangements. Those are useful starting points for a business specific receptionist.

Give the receptionist reliable information

Prepare accurate service descriptions, prices, operating hours, policies and handover instructions. The AI should answer from information you approve, not guess.

Keep a human involved for important decisions

Complex requests, complaints, negotiations and urgent issues may still need a staff member. Configure a clear handover path where your phone provider supports it.
