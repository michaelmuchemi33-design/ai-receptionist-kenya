AI receptionist for Kenyan businesses

AI receptionist for small businesses in Kenya

A practical first point of contact for small teams that need to handle customer calls while keeping their attention on daily work.

Make it easier for customers to reach your business

In a small business, the person answering the phone is often also serving customers, completing jobs, preparing orders or managing the business. Calls can arrive at the busiest moment or outside normal working hours. A configured AI receptionist can answer approved routine questions, collect an enquiry and help direct the caller to the right next step.

TWIGA is designed for Kenyan businesses that want a more organised way to handle first contact. The best starting point is not to automate every task. It is to identify the calls that interrupt the team most often and agree how those callers should be helped.

Useful first-response tasks

Answer common questions

Provide approved details about business hours, location, products, services, service areas and how customers can get started.

Capture new leads

Record the caller’s name, contact details and reason for calling so the right team member can follow up.

Collect order or booking enquiries

Gather the information needed for a human or connected system to review and confirm the request.

Route requests

Identify calls that need a salesperson, technician, manager or other staff member and use handover options supported by your phone setup.

Give clear business instructions

Prepare a short, accurate description of what the business does, what it sells, the services it provides, its prices and the areas it serves. Add opening hours, delivery or collection rules, booking policies and frequently asked questions. Make clear which topics need a staff member and which answers must never be guessed.

Choose a call flow that suits a small team

Decide how a call should be greeted, what information should be collected and who receives the enquiry. A repair business may need the equipment type and a short description of the problem. A retailer may need a product name and quantity. A professional service may need the type of help requested and a preferred time for a callback. Capture only what is necessary to respond.

Confirm what your telephone setup supports

Features such as forwarding from an existing number, transferring a caller, connecting a booking calendar or recording an interaction depend on the phone provider and configuration. Ask for a demonstration that uses your intended number and the scenarios you care about. Confirm how customer information is stored, who can access it and how long it is retained.

Improve the process after real conversations

Review a sample of enquiries with your team. Look for questions the receptionist could answer more clearly, requests that should be sent to a person and details that are no longer current. Use those findings to update the instructions. A simple system that staff can maintain is more useful than a long list of features the business does not need.

Frequently asked questions

Which small businesses can use an AI receptionist?

It can suit businesses that receive repeat customer enquiries, such as salons, shops, repair services, professional firms and hospitality businesses. The call flow should be tailored to each business.

Can it answer using my prices and product information?

Yes, when accurate information has been provided and approved. Set rules for discounts, custom quotations and availability that need staff confirmation.

Can it work with my current phone number?

That depends on the phone provider, SIM or call-forwarding options and required permissions. Confirm compatibility with a real test before switching customer calls.
