OVERVIEW
TWIGA helps Kenyan businesses organise first responses to customer calls, routine enquiries, appointment requests, order enquiries and follow-up tasks. Each business configures a private workspace with its own name, products, services, prices, hours, location, customer questions, booking and order rules, response language and human handover instructions.

HOW IT WORKS
1. The business creates a private workspace.
2. The owner describes the business and reviews the AI receptionist profile.
3. The owner adds current business facts and approved instructions.
4. The business connects a phone provider and line where supported.
5. The owner tests inbound calls and verifies phone line and handover before enabling live handling.
6. The business reviews enquiries, tasks and follow-up in its workspace.

POSSIBLE WORKFLOWS (SUBJECT TO CONFIGURATION)
- Answer routine questions using approved information.
- Capture caller name, callback number, reason for calling and request details.
- Record appointment or reservation requests.
- Collect order enquiries, product requests and quantities.
- Direct a caller to staff or create an owner follow-up task.
- Keep business records, enquiries and tasks scoped to the correct workspace.
- Support English and Kiswahili response preferences where configured and tested.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
- Workspace setup does not mean a phone number is connected or verified.
- Calls depend on the selected provider, assigned line, permissions and successful tests.
- A request is not a confirmed booking, order, payment, stock allocation or appointment.
- Live availability, prices, inventory, schedules and payment status must be verified by a current system or authorised person.
- TWIGA must not provide medical diagnosis, legal advice, financial advice or regulated professional decisions.
- Never ask customers for passwords, PINs, OTPs, full card details or credentials.
- Sensitive, urgent, emergency or disputed matters should be routed to the responsible human team.
