Which voices are available?

Available choices include Coral, Alloy, Ash, Ballad, Echo, Sage, Shimmer and Verse when supported by the selected model.

Can the receptionist understand my business?

You can configure it with information about your company, services, prices, working hours and frequently asked questions.

Can it collect customer details?

Yes, where the application is configured to capture relevant caller information.

Can it transfer a call to a human?

Human handover can be configured when supported by your telephone setup.

Can it book appointments?

It can collect appointment requests. Confirmed scheduling requires an appropriate calendar or booking integration.

Does it support English and Swahili?

Language performance depends on the selected AI model, voice and configuration. The supported language experience should be demonstrated before promising a particular level of fluency.

Does it replace human receptionists?

No. It is designed to support your team by handling routine enquiries and providing an additional point of contact.

How much does it cost?

The monthly packages are: Starter KSh 1,500, Professional KSh 3,000, Business Pro KSh 5,000. Telephone usage, number rental and AI processing may incur separate charges.

Can I change my subscription?

Customers can request a package upgrade or change subject to the available billing and subscription-management system.

How do I get started?

Choose your preferred package, provide your business information and arrange the setup of your AI receptionist.
