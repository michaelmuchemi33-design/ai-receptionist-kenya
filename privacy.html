WEBSITE PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

This page explains how information submitted through the AI Receptionist Kenya website is used when you request a demonstration or contact the team.

Information you submit

The demonstration form may ask for your name, business name, email address, telephone number, industry, preferred demonstration time and a message about your requirements. Please do not submit passwords, payment-card details, medical information or private information about your customers.

How submitted information is used

Information submitted through the website is sent to the website booking service so the team can review your request, contact you and prepare a relevant demonstration. Newsletter sign-up details, where enabled, are used to manage the requested email updates.

Service providers

The website uses hosting and database services to deliver the site and process form submissions. Those providers process information as needed to provide the service. This page does not claim a specific retention period or list every subprocessor.

Your choices and requests

You can choose not to submit the form. To ask about information you have submitted or request a correction, contact the team using the demonstration request page and include enough detail for the team to locate your request.

Updates

This notice may be updated when the website or its data practices change. Check this page for the current version before submitting information.

Questions about this page?

Contact the TWIGA team through the demonstration request page.
