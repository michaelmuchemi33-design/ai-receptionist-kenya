QUALITY RULES
- One main search intent per page.
- Do not make all pages target the exact same phrase.
- Each page needs unique scenarios, checklist, handover, FAQs and examples.
- Use verified local facts; do not fabricate testimonials, rankings or availability.
- Avoid mass-created near-duplicate industry and city pages.
- Keep titles, meta descriptions and canonical URLs unique.
- Make primary content crawlable HTML.

TECHNICAL CHECKLIST
- Canonical URL returns HTTP 200 and is indexable.
- Sitemap contains canonical, working pages only.
- robots.txt allows intended crawling.
- New pages are internally linked from relevant hubs.
- One H1, clear headings, unique title and description.
- Important text is HTML, not embedded in images or client-only content.
- JSON-LD matches visible content; do not add fake reviews.
- Mobile, accessibility and performance are checked.

GOOGLE AND AI SEARCH
Google Search Central says useful people-first content and crawlable site structure remain the foundation for AI Search features. Google explicitly says llms.txt and similar special files do not improve or harm Google rankings and are ignored by Google Search. They are optional reference files for systems that choose to read them, not ranking hacks.
