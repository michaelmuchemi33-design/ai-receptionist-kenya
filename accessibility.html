ACCESSIBILITY

Accessibility Statement

AI Receptionist Kenya aims to make its website usable across common desktop and mobile devices and to improve accessibility as issues are identified.

Our approach

We aim to use readable text, meaningful headings, descriptive image alternatives, keyboard-operable controls and layouts that adapt to smaller screens.

Known limitations

Some visual components originate from a responsive page export and may not behave identically in every browser, zoom level or assistive technology. We continue to review navigation, forms, images and page structure.

Request help or report a barrier

If you cannot access information or complete a form, contact the team through the demonstration request page and describe the page and issue. We will review the report and look for a practical way to help.

Questions about this page?

Contact the TWIGA team through the demonstration request page.
